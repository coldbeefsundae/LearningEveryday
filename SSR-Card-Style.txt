STARQUEST - SSR CARD STYLE GUIDE AND PROMPTS
Version 1 (Nov 11, 2026). Based on the 12 existing SSR cards.

==================================================================
1. WHAT AN SSR CARD IS
==================================================================
SSR is the top normal rarity. Each card is a big moment in history
(a person, legend, event, place or creature) told for an 8-year-old.
Every SSR card gets:
  - Square painted art in the house style below.
  - The rainbow SSR border, holo foil and SPARKLES (unchanged; the
    sparkles stay on every SSR card).
  - Its region's painted holographic frame on top (26.11.78+).
    The frame adds to the sparkles; it never replaces them.
    Frames: CN sumi ink arc, JP cherry branches, KR mother-of-pearl
    lattice, CA aurora (colour holo), EU Gothic/Gaudi/ivy,
    AM Art Deco/monarch/skyline, SA step-fret/vines/sun,
    AF woven filigree, AU ripples/shells/waves, AS multifoil
    arches/lotus, GL golden orbits.
  - SSSR cards later use the same regional frame.

==================================================================
2. THE ART STYLE (what the existing cards look like)
==================================================================
Medium:      Warm storybook illustration; gouache-and-watercolor look,
             fine ink outlines, soft painted texture.
Format:      Square. Art fills the card window.
Subject:     One clear main subject, centered and large. People are
             usually waist-up to knee-up, friendly and calm, kind
             faces, never fierce (even warriors look gentle).
Background:  A recognizable scene that tells the story at a glance
             (Qin: Great Wall + terracotta army; Newton: apple tree,
             prism rainbow, planets; Sejong: children reading).
Light:       Golden-hour glow, warm skies, sunset or lantern light.
Colour:      Rich, saturated but gentle. Lots of small charming
             details.
Extras:      Optional small cheerful children of many backgrounds
             (MLK, Sejong style).
Real people: Painted friendly and recognizable (as with MLK, Biles).
Never:       Text, letters, logos, flags as the focus, weapons in use,
             blood, injury, scary faces, horror, dark mood.

==================================================================
3. BASE PROMPT (paste at the start of every SSR image prompt)
==================================================================
Square storybook illustration in exactly the same style as the
reference cards: warm gouache-and-watercolor look with fine ink
outlines, soft painted texture, golden-hour glow, rich but gentle
saturated colors, charming small details. One clear main subject,
centered and large, friendly calm expression. Background is a
recognizable scene that tells the story at a glance. Child-friendly
for an 8-year-old: no text, no letters, no logos, no violence, no
scary elements. Leave a little calm space at the edges so a thin
decorative frame can sit on top without covering the subject.

Reference: always attach 2-4 existing SSR card images (or the
collection screenshot) as the style reference. Copy the style only,
never the composition.

==================================================================
4. CARD PROMPTS (base prompt + one of these)
==================================================================

--- CANADA: Chris Hadfield (Person, 1959-) ---
Tagline: "The astronaut who sang in space"
Prompt:
A smiling Canadian astronaut with a friendly moustache, floating
waist-up inside the round cupola window of the International Space
Station, wearing a simple blue flight shirt, holding an acoustic
guitar that floats gently beside him. Through the window: the curve
of planet Earth glowing blue and white, and a soft green and violet
aurora ribbon over the dark side. A few tiny floating water droplets
catch the light. Warm cabin light on his face.

--- ASIA & MIDEAST: Scheherazade and the Thousand and One Nights
    (Legend) ---
Tagline: "The storyteller who told a story every night"
Prompt:
A kind young storyteller woman in flowing jewel-toned robes and a
light head scarf, sitting on embroidered cushions, telling a story
with one hand raised, lit by a glowing brass oil lamp. Above her,
her stories swirl up like painted dream bubbles: a flying carpet,
a golden lamp with a friendly blue genie, a sailing ship, and a giant
roc bird with wide wings. Behind her, an arched window opens onto
domes and minarets of old Baghdad at dusk under the first stars.
No king, no palace guards, no weapons.

--- GLOBAL: The Moon Landing (Event, 1969) ---
Tagline: "One giant leap for all of us"
Prompt:
An astronaut in a white space suit with a golden visor (no face
visible) standing on the grey Moon surface beside a lunar lander,
waving. In the foreground, one clear boot print in the moon dust.
The Earth rises large, blue and white over the horizon against a
starry sky. Soft golden light on the suit and dust. No visible flag
text or words.

--- SOUTH AMERICA: The First Chocolate (Event, c. 3300 BC,
    Ecuador) ---
Tagline: "Chocolate began in the Amazon"
Prompt:
In a misty golden Amazon rainforest, a large glowing cacao pod split
open in the center showing its creamy seeds, resting beside simple
ancient decorated clay pots. A friendly person from long ago
(softly painted, kind face, simple clothing) gently stirs a frothy
cacao drink in a clay bowl. Colorful parrots and a blue morpho
butterfly nearby, big leaves, sun rays through the canopy.

--- AUSTRALIA & OCEANIA: The Platypus (Creature/Place, Australia)
    ---
Tagline: "Scientists thought it was a fake!"
Prompt:
A cheerful platypus swimming through sunlit river ripples, duck
bill forward, webbed feet paddling, a trail of bubbles behind it and
a faint soft shimmer of light around its bill (its sense for
electricity). Gum trees and a grassy riverbank at golden hour, a
kookaburra on a branch, and two small cheerful children peeking from
the bank in wonder.

==================================================================
5. SAFETY RULES (for an 8-year-old)
==================================================================
- Gentle tone only. Hard history is told softly and ends hopefully.
- No weapons in use, no injury, no frightening creatures or faces.
- Sacred or cultural symbols are not used as decoration; culture is
  shown through respectful everyday scenes and named in the text.
- No text inside images (the card adds its own name and tag).
- Original generations only; never copy an artist's or
  photographer's work.
- Nothing personal about the family in any prompt, file name or
  image (the repo is public).

==================================================================
6. WORKFLOW
==================================================================
1. Scope the card (name, local name, year, type, emoji, tagline,
   story idea) and get approval.
2. Generate art with: base prompt + card prompt + style reference.
3. Review drafts with Victor before anything goes into the app.
4. Write card text and the 8-year-old story with read-aloud lines.
5. Add to the app as a hashed asset (assets/cards/), update
   assets.json, keep the overall SSR pull rate the same.
6. The regional frame and sparkles appear automatically.
