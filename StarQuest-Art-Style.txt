STARQUEST - GENERAL ART STYLE GUIDE
Version 1 (Nov 11, 2026). Applies to all card art, story pictures and
LL scenes. Based on the existing SSR cards and the LL card pictures.
(Lower-rarity cards C/B/A/S were not reviewed for this version; check
a few and note any differences before relying on this for them.)

==================================================================
1. THE LOOK IN ONE SENTENCE
==================================================================
A warm, glowing storybook painting: gouache-and-watercolor look with
fine ink outlines, soft painted texture, golden light, rich but
gentle colors, and lots of small charming details. Friendly, calm
and curious, never scary.

==================================================================
2. CORE STYLE RULES
==================================================================
Medium
  - Hand-painted storybook feel: gouache and watercolor, soft
    dappled brushwork, visible paper texture.
  - Fine, clean ink outlines around main shapes.
  - Never photoreal, never 3D-render, never flat vector or anime.

Light
  - Golden hour, sunset, lantern, lamp or soft daylight glow.
  - Warm highlights on faces and edges; soft shadows.
  - Night scenes are deep blue with warm points of light, not black.

Color
  - Rich, saturated but gentle. Warm skies (peach, gold, rose) with
    blue/green balance.
  - No muddy, grey or gloomy palettes.

Composition
  - One clear main subject, easy to read at thumbnail size.
  - Subject centered and large on cards; people usually waist-up to
    knee-up.
  - Background tells the story at a glance with 1-3 recognizable
    clues (landmark, object, symbol of the achievement).
  - Leave calm space at the edges (frames, borders and UI sit there).

People
  - Kind, friendly faces and calm, confident poses.
  - Real historical or living people painted friendly and
    recognizable.
  - Optional small cheerful children of many backgrounds as
    onlookers or learners.
  - In story scenes and LL pictures, other people appear as small,
    softly painted figures (no close-up faces of strangers).

Detail
  - Charming small touches (lanterns, birds, blossoms, tools, books)
    that reward a second look, without clutter around the subject.

==================================================================
3. BY USE
==================================================================
Card art (all rarities)
  - Square. Subject + story background as above.
  - Rarity effects (borders, foil, holo, sparkles, SSR regional
    frames) are added by the app, NOT painted into the art.

SSR cards
  - Same style, the most "hero moment" composition and glow.
  - See SSR-Card-Style.txt for the prompts and frame notes.

LL (Living Legendary) story pictures
  - Use the card art as the style reference for every shot.
  - Stills with camera pans/zooms/glows, not AI video (style drift).
  - See LL-Card-Playbook.md for the full build guide.

Story and scene pictures
  - Same style; wider scenes are fine; people small and soft.

==================================================================
4. BASE PROMPT (paste at the start of any StarQuest image prompt)
==================================================================
Storybook illustration in exactly the same art style as the reference
pictures: warm gouache-and-watercolor look with fine ink outlines,
soft dappled brushwork and visible paper texture, golden glowing
light, rich but gentle saturated colors, charming small details.
Friendly, calm, curious tone for an 8-year-old. One clear main
subject that reads well when small, with a background that tells the
story at a glance. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no
violence, no scary elements. Copy the reference style only, never
its composition.

Add for card art:
Square format. Main subject centered and large with a friendly calm
expression. Leave a little calm space at the edges so a border or
frame can sit on top without covering the subject.

Add for story/LL scenes:
Other people appear only as small, softly painted figures. Match the
card art's palette and light exactly so the shots feel continuous.

Reference: always attach 2-4 existing StarQuest pictures as the
style reference (card art for cards, the card itself for its story).

==================================================================
5. NEVER
==================================================================
- Text, letters, captions, logos, watermarks, UI.
- Weapons in use, blood, injury, death, fear, horror, dark mood.
- Sacred or cultural symbols used as decoration; culture is shown
  through respectful everyday scenes and named in the card text.
- Copying a real artist's or photographer's work or a famous
  painting's composition.
- Flags or emblems as the main focus.
- Anything personal about the family (the repo is public).

==================================================================
6. QUICK CHECK BEFORE APPROVING A PICTURE
==================================================================
[ ] Looks like it belongs next to the existing cards
[ ] Main subject readable at thumbnail size
[ ] Warm light, gentle colors, kind faces
[ ] Story clue visible in the background
[ ] No text or hidden letters
[ ] Edges calm enough for borders/frames
[ ] Nothing an 8-year-old would find scary or confusing
