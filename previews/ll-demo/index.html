Tap a card, then Watch. Put a finger on the card and drag: it tilts with you, and the holo, shine and sparkles follow (it drifts slowly on its own when nobody touches it). The glass breaks into 22 pieces, the card's own story plays with its music, sound and sparkles, then the glass slowly reforms (4 seconds) with layered chimes while the music plays out, fading over half a second as the card settles. Kowloon Walled City plays the new still-picture story. The other five play the stories already in StarQuest.
